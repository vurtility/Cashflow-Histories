# Episode 2: topic shortlist (Nov 2026)

Goal: maximum earnings = RPM × views. Scored with the skill's Stage 0 factors (1–5) plus an RPM tier.

**RPM caveats.** The ranges are planning estimates from published niche benchmarks. They conflict widely (insurance and credit cards run from about $7 to $40+), so treat vidIQ's as the conservative baseline. A video's topic shifts which ads it attracts, but channel audience and geography matter as much. Q4 (Oct–Dec) and January (money resolutions) pay the most. Replace these with our own YouTube Studio RPM once Subway has a few weeks of data.

## Recommendation: #1 (airlines)
It has the best RPM × views combination:
- A universal topic with a proven 2M comparable.
- Credit-card advertisers drive the RPM.
- A fresh news peg: Spirit's shutdown.
- A built-in twin/foil story (Spirit vs Delta).
- Primary data straight from 10-Ks: Delta's American Express revenue, and United and American's loyalty-programme disclosures.
- It extends Subway's theme naturally: "the business isn't what you think it is".

**Caveat to handle in the script:** the Amex payment is revenue, not pure profit (miles carry a redemption cost). So the claim must be "worth 1.4× Delta's operating profit", not "Delta loses money flying", unless segment data supports it.
