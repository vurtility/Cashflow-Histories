## Description

In 2015, Spirit Airlines made more profit on every dollar than Delta did. On 2 May 2026, it cancelled every flight it had. Delta, which went bankrupt in 2005, now gets more from American Express each year than its entire operating profit.

So how did the airline that was winning die, while the one that went bankrupt thrived? It wasn't the planes or the service. It's in your wallet.
