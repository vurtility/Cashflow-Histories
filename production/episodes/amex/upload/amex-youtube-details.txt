TITLE
Why People Pay $895 for a Credit Card

DESCRIPTION
In 2025, American Express raised its Platinum card's fee to $895 a year, and new sign-ups doubled. That same year it gave its customers $24.5 billion in rewards and perks, and still made $10.8 billion.

So who is actually paying? The answer goes back to a 1963 fraud involving tanks of salad oil, and the young Warren Buffett, who saw what the scandal couldn't touch.
