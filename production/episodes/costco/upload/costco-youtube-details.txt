TITLE
Costco Doesn't Make Money Selling Groceries

DESCRIPTION
Costco's hot dog and soda cost $1.50 in 1985, and they still do. Its $4.99 chicken reportedly loses money. And yet Costco made $8.1 billion of profit last year.

So how does a store that won't raise the price of a hot dog make billions? The answer is the card in your wallet.
