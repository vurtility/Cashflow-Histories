TITLE
Your Car Insurance Went Up. Progressive Made $11 Billion.

DESCRIPTION
At the end of 2023, car insurance in America cost 20% more than a year earlier, the biggest jump since 1976. Progressive's profit went from $722 million in 2022 to $11.3 billion in 2025. In Florida, it made so much that the law forces it to give nearly a billion dollars back.

So what happened to your bill? It's both inflation and profit, in that order.
