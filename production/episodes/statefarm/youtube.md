## Description

In July 2024, State Farm dropped about 1,600 home policies in Pacific Palisades. Six months later, the Palisades burned. State Farm is worth $170 billion and made $12.9 billion last year, yet it still won't sell a new home policy anywhere in California.

It isn't because it can't afford the fires. The answer is a company inside the company, a 1988 law, and the fact that State Farm loses money on home insurance across the whole country.
